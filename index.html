Inventory Calculations Used in This System:

  • Reorder Point (ROP): (Average Daily Demand × Lead Time Days) + Safety Buffer Stock
    Indicates the exact stock quantity at which a new order must be placed before running out.

  • Stock Depletion Runout (Days Left): Current Stock ÷ Average Daily Demand
    Estimates how many business days remain until the item runs out of stock completely.

  • 30-Day Demand Forecast: Average Daily Demand × 30 Days
    Projects retail customer purchases over the next 1-month sales cycle.

  • Suggested Reorder Quantity (EOQ estimate): (30-Day Forecast + Safety Stock) − Current Stock
    Calculates exact replacement units needed to restore safe inventory levels.
